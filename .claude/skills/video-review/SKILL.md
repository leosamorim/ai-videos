# Review the artifact

For natural talking-head edits, use [editorial craft](../../../docs/CRAFT.md) to evaluate redundancy, source-faithful diagrams and breathing room. Inspect `06-review/captions.json` for short/fast blocks; its flags are review prompts, not automatic failures. Separate measured results, inspected frames and actual playback/listening in the report.

Run `vid verify <slug>` for technical checks and `--speech` for optional local re-transcription compared with the planned speech. Inspect differences: recognizers can mistake units, names and short phrases. `--provider elevenlabs` sends audio externally only when explicitly selected/authorized. Existing local transcription remains the default.

Run `vid frames --cuts`, `--cards` or `--at` as appropriate, open outputs, and inspect motion around problems. For every animated element (draw-ons, transitions, moving inserts) also run `vid frames --seq START-END`, with `--crop` for small details: consecutive frames show whether the animation progresses, which spaced samples cannot. `vid snapshot` shows composition layout before a render, never motion. Listen or use the available audio/video review capability; ASR and loudness numbers do not establish pleasant sound or natural delivery. Record method and limits honestly.

Review the opening, every seam and ending according to [cut finishing](../../../docs/CUTTING.md). `frames --cuts` is a static locator; without playback, use `--cuts --context 0.5` on the exact render for consecutive frames (optionally `--boundary start|c01|end` to limit a call). Increase context when needed; it is not automatic cut padding. Record each boundary's evidence, visual/audio observation, decision and limitations in `checks.seams.notes` or a linked local report tied to that render hash. Preserve meaningful gaze, expression, silence and breath; remove demonstrated recording leftovers without clipping useful speech or action. Frame-only review cannot certify the sound.

Use `vid review` to prepare review.json. Cover:

- meaning: the intended explanation remains truthful, complete and coherent;
- performance: preparation/false starts/unintended post-roll are removed with evidence; useful gestures and reactions remain;
- seams: per-boundary findings for opening, internal seams and ending; no clipped syllables, accidental action truncation or unexplained recording resets, with listening limitations stated;
- captions: corrected Portuguese, dimensions/units, mobile readability and no collision with face/hands/graphics;
- visuals: helpful explanation, faithful reference use, readable transitions, HDR-to-SDR appearance and preserved crop detail;
- audio: voice intelligibility, suitable level, fades and music balance; process only demonstrated problems;
- references: displayed assets/claims match their sources, illustrative imagery is not presented as project evidence.
- strategy (when strategy.json exists): identify the delivered payoff in the render, compare it with the opening promise, check visibility of evidence, audience relevance and CTA fit. Check that effects clarify or carry meaning. Record concrete observations rather than predicting virality; a changed strategy needs a fresh review even if the video file is identical.

Run `review --complete` after filling observations. Assisted mode records actual user approval with `gate draft --approve`; autonomous mode completes within existing authorization. Changes invalidate the relevant review. A high-quality render needs its own inspection even after approving a lower-quality draft.

For localized revisions, follow [incremental review](../../../docs/EFFICIENCY.md): keep the previous exact-render review, run `vid diff --from ... --review ...` and inspect `frames --diff REPORT`. The full-resolution frame/PCM comparison must match timeline and visual metadata; inspect complete affected animations plus boundaries. Maintain a complete `motion-plan.json`, including authored motion outside helpers. Document which previously opened evidence is transferred and why. Missing motion inventory or prior review requires the normal inspection. Pixel identity does not establish semantic adequacy after a strategy/claim change, and altered audio needs fresh listening. The diff never grants approval.

`verify --speech` caches only the recognized audio; comparison against the current expected words is recalculated. Use logs/reports for anomalies instead of reopening all unchanged evidence. Do not claim observations from sheets merely generated by the command.

`finalize` refuses stale/unreviewed files and draft-quality masters, normalizes audio in two passes and verifies the delivered file. Publish/upload only when requested. Report the file, checks and any real limitations, without claiming that technical tests prove artistic quality.

When the user gives specific style feedback, preserve the relevant example and context through `vid remember` ([format](../../../library/SOURCES.md)). Record the actual feedback, time range and what to keep/avoid. Agent review can also be remembered, with `feedback.by: agent`. Neither autonomous completion nor technical validation constitutes human preference; do not automatically mark all final renders preferred.
